# Adapt

Read when distilling the article into a page plan and note copy. Adapt is the redesign step — it decides what the deck says, and just as importantly what it drops.

## The page plan

Target ≤12 pages unless the platform's limit or attention budget says otherwise. Shape:

1. **Cover** — series/EP marker if one exists, title + EN subtitle, one-line promise, topic chips, author line.
2. **Body pages** — one idea per page. A page answers one question or carries one derivation; if a section needs two, split it instead of shrinking the type.
3. **Closing** — the takeaway compressed to a checklist, then a CTA pointing at the full post.

What survives:

- The single sharpest insight gets a page or a callout — for a methods post that's usually the non-obvious caveat ("K/N 到底省不省算力取决于拆分方式"), not the definition.
- Formulas that *are* the payload stay as formulas. Derivations compress to their load-bearing steps.
- Code only where it teaches: ≤15 lines per block, trimmed to the mechanism (dispatch loop, not boilerplate `__init__`).
- One diagram per page max, and only where prose can't carry the structure (token→router→expert flow, shared vs routed experts).
- A worked numeric example beats a second paragraph of explanation — keep it if the article has one.

What drops: narrative transitions, repeated motivation, bibliography details (folded into a single closing list), and anything a reader can get by tapping the source link.

## The note copy

Write in the voice the task card recorded — from the account's posts or the supplied references, never guessed. With no references, use the neutral house style and say so in the report. Conventions observed on Xiaohongshu (the classic case — adapt them to the clarified platform):

- **Title** short and factual — xhs ≤20 chars, no clickbait suffixes. Match the reference pattern (`如何把游戏本变成服务器` → `图解 X 技术原理` / `一文搞懂 X`).
- **Body** skeleton: opening context line (`如题` / series position / why now) → the sharp insight stated plainly → bullet digest of what the note covers → `博客地址：<url>` → a soft closing line. Keep it under the platform's comfortable length.
- **Topics** — attach real topic/tag entities through the platform's mechanism (e.g. `xhs topics <kw>` to resolve before posting); don't freetype hashtags into the body unless that's the platform's convention.

## Presenting the plan

Emit the plan as a table — `page | title | carries` — plus the full title/body/topics draft. In `align first` mode, stop here and wait. In the default mode, proceed to Layout and let the preview post carry the review.
